Who We Are

PhishGuard is an educational cybersecurity project designed to help users identify suspicious messages, website links and mobile numbers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to spread awareness about online fraud and phishing attacks and encourage safer digital habits.

Why PhishGuard?

Online scams can affect anyone. PhishGuard provides simple security checks and warning signs to help users make informed decisions before trusting suspicious content.
